In 2022, I was selected to be a part of the PJ2Y Ham Radio team to fly out to Curacao and operate at a world class station. within the few days we were there, we broke the record for amount of contacts made for a youth group. I was able to learn many technical skills relating to amplifiers, RF, antennas, azmiuth caclucation, and more. This was the experience of a lifetime.

When I have time during breaks from school, I find myself working with my Raspberry Pi 4, a 4 relay switch, and an EDS 1115. This is all in coordination to automize and make my ham radio station remote. I am focusing on components such as the antenna rotator and antenna switcher, and these are some of the most needed components for my Ham Radio station. I am using Node-Red to create an interface that allows me to control all of these options remotely by connecting to a VPN and my Rapsberry Pi.

During my time at Parsons Corporation, I participated on a project that optimized python scripts that track CDM's and space debris. We filtered out all targets that are smaller than a basketball, then plotted the data for those targst that were larger.
